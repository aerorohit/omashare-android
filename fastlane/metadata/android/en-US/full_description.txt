Omashare sends files between your devices. A Linux computer and an Android phone, or two phones. There is no account. The other person accepts before a file is saved.

Turn sharing on while you are exchanging files, then turn it off. Nearby devices appear from the local network or from Bluetooth. Bluetooth is only used to discover a device. The file is transferred over the network.

Share from another app by choosing Omashare in the share sheet. Accepted files are saved in Downloads/Omashare.
